Remote Desktop Toggle

A menu-bar switch to turn Chrome Remote Desktop access on and off without uninstalling it or redoing setup.

  • Off: your Mac goes offline in Chrome Remote Desktop and stays off after a restart. No password needed.
  • On: back online in seconds with your existing PIN. Asks for your password or Touch ID.

This installs:

  • /Applications/Remote Desktop Toggle.app
  • A small helper in /Library/PrivilegedHelperTools/remote-desktop-toggle/
  • A sudoers rule in /etc/sudoers.d/remote-desktop-toggle that allows only the “turn off” action without a password

Chrome and Chrome Remote Desktop themselves are not modified.
